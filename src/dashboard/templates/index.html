Operational GKMS Implementation Rules

1. Irrigation Trigger: Cumulative 3-day rainfall < 2.5 mm during vegetative growth triggers light irrigation scheduling.

2. Heavy Rain Drainage: 24-hour downscaled rainfall > 50 mm triggers urgent drainage channel clearance to prevent nutrient leaching.

3. Pest & Fungal Risk: Max temp > 35°C combined with RH > 70% activates Downy Mildew and Thrip preventive spraying alerts.

4. Wind Hazard: Wind gusts > 40 km/h enforce earthing-up and strict suspension of foliar pesticide spraying.
